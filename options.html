silly searcher.

A new way to find.
With your own key.

Paste a TypeSafe API key from console.typesafe.ai. The key stays in this browser's extension storage, unencrypted and unsynced, and is only ever sent to TypeSafe's API. No backend, no middleman.

Click the Silly Searcher toolbar icon on a webpage, or press ⌘⇧F on Mac / Ctrl+Shift+F elsewhere.

Search sends the page's readable passages and your query directly to TypeSafe Jev. Content inside iframes and shadow DOM is not searched. Chrome system pages and built-in PDF viewers are not supported. Based on Shubham Saboo's Needle (github.com/Shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps).
